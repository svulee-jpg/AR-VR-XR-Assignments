Choose your apartment's finish in VR

Demo project: how a developer could show buyers their finishing options before any renovation starts. The apartment is still raw. Choose what it will become.

How it works

  • Pick a ready-made design (Scandinavian, Loft, Classic and more) or build your own: walls, accent wall, floor, ceiling, skirting board and door.
  • Every surface has materials (paint, brick, microcement, herringbone parquet, porcelain tile...) and dozens of colours. In total there are … combinations.
  • Look at a wall or the floor for a second and it gets selected in VR. You can also click with the mouse or say, for example: "red brick walls", "dark walnut herringbone floor", "loft design".
  • Walk around and look in every direction: drag the mouse to look 360°, W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, Q / E to turn. In a headset: left stick walks, right stick turns. The panel follows you.
  • The "Done" button shows the total cost, the monthly mortgage payment and a variant code you can share.
What you need to allow: the microphone (optional).
It is only used to change the finishes with your voice (English). While recognition is on, Chrome sends the audio to Google's speech recognition service. This app does not record or store audio and does not save any personal data. The camera and location are not used.

Student demo project of a fictional developer. Prices are made up and are not an offer. Works best in desktop Chrome (voice) and in VR mode (headset button at the bottom right). If the microphone is not allowed or does not work, everything is still available by gaze and buttons. Controls: mouse drag (look), W A S D / arrows (walk), Q / E (turn).
